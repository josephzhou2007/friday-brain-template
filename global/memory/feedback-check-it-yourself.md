name: Check it yourself
description: Verify what you can before asking the owner
metadata:
  type: feedback
---
Anything that can be confirmed by reading a file, a log, a page or a screenshot should be
checked by the agent first; report the conclusion with the evidence.

**Why:** a busy owner asked again and again for facts the agent could look up stops using the agent.

**How to apply:** only ask the owner for what only they can do (approve on their phone,
enter a password, pay, publish) or decisions that are theirs, and ask as few as possible at
once. This does not widen permissions: sending, deleting and payments still need their OK.
